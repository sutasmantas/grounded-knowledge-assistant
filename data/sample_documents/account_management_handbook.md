# Account Management Handbook

## Approval thresholds

Commercial exceptions with less than 50,000 USD in remaining contract value
require written approval from the Account Director and Finance. Legal approval
is also required when the remaining value is 50,000 USD or more, when contract
language is unclear, or when the request creates a non-standard liability.

## Account record

The Account Owner attaches the request, supporting evidence and named approvals
to the account record. Operations verifies that the approval set is complete
before changing contract dates or sending instructions to Billing.

## Security-related requests

Security-related termination requests require a confirmed incident record.
Security and Legal review the record before the Account Director decides whether
to sponsor a commercial exception.

## Request intake

The Account Owner creates one request record for each proposed commercial
change. The record identifies the customer, contract, requested outcome,
requested date and business sponsor. Related email may be attached, but the
request fields remain the source of truth. Duplicate requests are linked and
closed rather than reviewed independently.

## Evidence quality

Evidence must be dated, attributable and relevant to the requested outcome.
Screenshots without a visible source, unsigned draft amendments and summaries
that cannot be traced to a customer message are not sufficient approval
evidence. The Account Owner may ask the customer for clarification before
routing the request.

## Escalation route

If required approvers disagree, the request remains pending. The Account
Director may escalate commercial questions to the regional commercial lead,
but the escalation does not replace Finance or Legal approval. Urgency,
forecast pressure and an approaching renewal date do not authorize Operations
to bypass the recorded approval set.

## Handoff to Operations

After approval, the Account Owner posts a concise implementation instruction
containing the approved effective date, affected order and customer-notice
owner. Operations compares the instruction with the approval record. Any
difference in value, date or liability terms returns the request to the Account
Owner instead of being interpreted by Operations.

## Routine account changes

Contact updates, billing-contact replacements and internal account-team changes
do not use the commercial-exception workflow when they leave contract value,
dates and liability unchanged. The Account Owner still records the change so
future notices and renewal tasks reach the correct people.
