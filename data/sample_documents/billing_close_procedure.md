# Billing Close Procedure

## Early termination

Operations must record the effective date, reason and named approvers before
Billing schedules a final invoice. Billing validates open credits, usage charges
and prepaid balances, then records the final invoice identifier on the account.

## Refund routing

Approved refunds return to the original payment method. Finance validates the
payment and workspace-usage evidence before releasing funds.

## Completion

The renewal is closed only after the final invoice or refund has been recorded
and the Account Owner confirms that the customer received written notice.

## Close checklist

The close record contains the contract identifier, approved effective date,
service end date, final invoice owner and customer-notice evidence. Billing
does not infer missing dates from a support ticket. If the approval and
implementation instruction disagree, the close is paused and returned to
Operations.

## Usage reconciliation

Usage charges are calculated through the approved service end date. Late usage
files are attached to the same close record and reviewed before an invoice is
released. A usage estimate may support an accrual but cannot be presented to
the customer as the final measured quantity.

## Credit treatment

Open service credits are first applied according to the order form. Credits
that are promotional, expired or restricted to another product are not
converted into cash. Finance documents any manual credit decision separately
from a subscription refund so the two controls are not confused.

## Invoice corrections

An incorrect legal name, tax identifier or purchase-order reference is handled
as an invoice correction. A correction does not reopen a terminated service or
change the approved end date. Changes to price, quantity or the contracted
term require the commercial approval process.

## Billing-close service levels

| Close condition | Target action |
| --- | --- |
| Complete approval and usage record | Prepare final invoice within 3 business days |
| Missing usage evidence | Pause and request reconciliation |
| Approved refund | Route to Finance within 2 business days |
| Contract or effective-date conflict | Return to Operations |

## Audit retention

Billing retains the close checklist, calculation support, invoice, refund
record and delivery evidence for seven years. Internal working notes may be
removed after the close is complete if the required evidence remains attached
to the account record.
