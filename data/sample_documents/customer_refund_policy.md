# Customer Refund Policy

## Annual subscriptions

An annual subscription is eligible for a refund when the request is submitted
within 14 days of the initial purchase and workspace usage remains below 25
active-user sessions. Renewals and plans with completed onboarding services are
not eligible under this rule.

## Validation

Finance validates the payment date and usage report. The Account Owner confirms
that onboarding services were not delivered. Approved refunds return to the
original payment method.

## Request contents

A refund request includes the workspace identifier, purchase date, payment
reference, requester identity and stated reason. The submission date is the
date the complete request reaches the billing channel. A general complaint or
product-feedback message does not start the refund review unless it asks for a
refund.

## Usage calculation

An active-user session is counted when a named user signs in and completes an
authenticated product action. Automated health checks, failed sign-ins and
invited users who never sign in are excluded. Finance uses the workspace usage
export generated at review time and retains it with the decision.

## Refund eligibility matrix

| Scenario | Standard refund |
| --- | --- |
| Initial annual purchase, day 14, 24 sessions | Eligible |
| Initial annual purchase, day 15 | Not eligible |
| Initial annual purchase, 25 sessions | Not eligible |
| Renewal payment | Not eligible |
| Completed onboarding service | Not eligible |

## Duplicate and partial requests

Duplicate requests for the same payment are linked to the first open review.
The standard rule does not provide prorated refunds after the eligibility
window. A separate approved commercial remedy may create a partial credit, but
it is recorded as an exception and must not be labeled a standard refund.

## Fraud and payment disputes

When the payment owner reports fraud or an active chargeback, Finance pauses the
ordinary refund workflow and follows the payment-dispute procedure. The
Account Owner must not promise both a refund and a chargeback outcome for the
same payment.

## Decision notice

The Account Owner sends the customer the eligibility decision and, when
approved, the expected payment-processing time. The notice avoids exposing
internal risk notes or personal data from the usage report.
