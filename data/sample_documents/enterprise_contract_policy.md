# Enterprise Contract Policy

## Committed term

Enterprise orders remain active for the committed term stated in the order form.
Convenience cancellation is not available during that period. Early termination
is available only where the order form contains an express termination right or
an authorized commercial exception has been approved.

## Notice

When an express termination right applies, the customer must provide written
notice at least 60 days before the requested termination date. Operations records
the notice and links it to the account record.

## Commercial exceptions

Commercial exceptions must document the business reason, remaining contract
value, effective date and required approvers. The Account Director owns the
request and Operations must not change the renewal date before approval.

## Renewal administration

Orders renew only when the order form contains an automatic-renewal clause.
Operations sends the Account Owner a review task 120 days before the renewal
date. The review records the proposed term, current product package and any
pricing commitment. A forecast note is not a customer notice and does not
change the contract.

## Product and quantity changes

Customers may add products or quantities during an active term through a signed
order amendment. Reductions normally take effect at renewal unless the order
form provides a mid-term adjustment right. Sales notes, support tickets and
verbal statements are not amendments. Operations records the signed amendment
and its effective date before provisioning changes are requested.

## Assignment and ownership changes

A customer request to transfer an agreement to another legal entity requires
the proposed entity name, registration country and effective date. Legal
reviews assignment restrictions and Finance checks outstanding balances. An
internal account rename is not an assignment and does not require this review.

## Notice channels

Contract notices are sent to the notice addresses in the order form. Ordinary
service email, chat and support portals may be used for operational updates but
do not replace a required written legal notice. The account record stores the
original notice, delivery evidence and the date received.

## Data export at end of service

After an approved termination becomes effective, the customer has 30 days to
request a standard data export. The export window does not extend the contract
term or delay the final invoice. Security may restrict an export while a legal
hold or confirmed account-compromise investigation remains open.
