# People Operations Handbook

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely from an approved country for up to 30 consecutive
days with manager and People Operations approval. Requests involving tax,
immigration or regulated-data access require additional review.

## Equipment

Company equipment remains company property and must use managed security
controls. Lost devices must be reported to the service desk immediately.

## Leave

Planned leave should be submitted through the HR system and approved by the
employee's manager before travel is booked.

## Remote-work request contents

The request states the destination country, dates, working address and whether
the employee will access regulated data. The manager confirms coverage and
People Operations checks the location. Approval for one trip does not create
standing approval for later travel.

## Review triggers

People Operations routes the request for specialist review when the trip may
create payroll, tax, immigration, export-control or employment-law exposure.
Security reviews work involving regulated data or a location with elevated
device risk. The employee must wait for the additional review before working
from that location.

## Working-time expectations

Employees remain responsible for their agreed working hours, availability and
time recording. A remote-work approval does not convert working days into
leave. Managers document any temporary schedule change and ensure that team
coverage remains clear.

## Equipment preparation

Before travel, employees install required updates and confirm that disk
encryption and endpoint monitoring are active. Sensitive work should not be
performed on shared public computers. Suspected device compromise is reported
through the same urgent service-desk channel used for a lost device.

## Travel and expenses

Temporary remote work is employee-initiated unless a business assignment says
otherwise. Personal travel, accommodation and connectivity are not reimbursed
under this policy. Business travel follows the separate travel-and-expense
process.

## Return and extension

An employee who needs more than 30 consecutive days submits a new request
before the approved period ends. Managers cannot extend the period informally.
After returning, the employee reports any equipment or access issue discovered
during travel.
